PiP Anywhere helper for Windows
===============================

Keeps a Chrome/Edge/Brave window always on top and lets you change its opacity,
together with the PiP Anywhere browser extension.

Install:   double-click install.cmd   (no administrator rights needed)
Uninstall: double-click uninstall.cmd

Files are installed to %LOCALAPPDATA%\PipAnywhere and registered under
HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\<browser>\NativeMessagingHosts\com.pipanywhere.host.
Log: %LOCALAPPDATA%\PipAnywhere\host.log
